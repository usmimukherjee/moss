Make quiet mode real

A normal website can’t control your other tabs. The included Chrome/Edge extension can—and it only blocks the sites you choose while a focus session is running.

  1. Download or open the extension folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose “Load unpacked” and select that folder.

No accounts. No analytics. Your intentions and history stay on your device.
